# Mega XO — deferred game roadmap

These items are intentionally **not part of V3.4**. Keeping them here prevents future work from accidentally mixing them into the V3.4 balancing/season release.

## Future: Match History + Replays

Product intent: a premium/monetizable strategy-analysis feature.

Candidate scope:
- completed match list with opponent, mode, result, duration, rating delta and currency result;
- tap a match to reconstruct the Mega Board and all nine Mini Boards;
- step forward/back through the authoritative move sequence;
- visualize each routing decision and forced destination;
- shareable replay/deep link;
- optional analysis layer later.

V3.4 rule: aggregate Stats remain available, but no user-facing move-history/replay product is introduced.

## Future: Daily Tactical Puzzles

Product intent: teach Mega XO routing strategy and create a lightweight daily-return loop.

Candidate scope:
- "Find the winning route" positions;
- forced-win / defensive-save / routing-trap categories;
- daily puzzle streak and optional quest integration;
- puzzle positions can later be curated or generated from real matches;
- no effect on Ranked Elo.

V3.4 rule: no puzzle economy, streak or new currency is introduced.

## Deliberately not scheduled

A sixth core matchmaking mode, power-ups, pay-to-win board advantages, and Elo modifiers based on purchases are not planned. Ranked skill remains separated from wealth.
