## Deliberate changes requiring a product decision before release

Purchased currency is not allowed into stakes. Coins cannot be bought, converted from Crowns or cashed out. Crowns are cosmetic-only. This is a risk-reduction architecture, not a legal determination that earned-coin stakes are permissible everywhere.

No fake online activity or live financial integration is included to make a checklist look complete. The missing authentication, multiplayer and transactional backend are clearly visible in the UI and README.
