# V3.4 Economy Simulation Results

This is a deterministic pressure model for balancing. It is **not** a forecast of retention, conversion rate, ARPU, or revenue.

## Why the V3.3 economy was changed

The previous reward schedule minted currency much faster than the core sinks removed it. In the corrected 90-day model, the legacy policy still produces a median wallet above 11,000 Coin-equivalent and more than 146 newly generated Coins per player per day. That would make currency progressively less meaningful and make the Coin -> Crown exchange too easy to farm at scale.

V3.4 therefore keeps the reduced recurring minting:
- Starting wallet: **150 Coins**
- Daily quest maximum: **30 Coins**
- Bot daily reward cap: **20 Coins**
- Bot rewarded wins: **3 per difficulty**
- Ranked win bonus: **60% of the league ticket**, rounded down, with a **50 Coin daily cap**
- Weekly league rewards: 20 / 25 / 30 / 40 / 50 / 65 / 85 / 110 / 150 / 220 / 300
- Low tournament: **100 Coins**
- Medium tournament: **400 Coins**
- High tournament: **1,200 Coins**
- Premium tournament: **200 Crowns**
- Coin/Crown exchange remains **10:1 both ways**
- Ranked queue ticket prices are unchanged
- Direct ranked challenge 50/50 payout/burn is unchanged

## Population model

5,000 deterministic modeled players are simulated for 90 days across four behavioral archetypes: light, core, competitive and high-stakes.

The corrected model now mirrors the important runtime constraints that were previously simplified:
- the Ranked win bonus is capped per player per day, including the legacy comparator;
- Coin-funded activities may use existing Crowns through the real 10:1 Crown -> Coin conversion;
- modeled purchasers may buy Crown packs and then convert them to Coins when continuing a Coin-denominated activity;
- purchase **need** and simulated **purchase conversion** are separate metrics;
- direct challenge minimums use the real tier-gap quote function, with a modeled mix of minimum, 1.5x and 2x voluntary stakes;
- weekly rewards require the real 5 rated games / 3 queue games / 3 opponents / 3 active days qualification instead of a random weekly payout.

The model uses conservative behavioral purchase propensities only after a buyer-intent archetype reaches a shortfall: 5% for core Ranked continuation, 10% for Coin tournaments, 20% for direct challenges and 25% for Premium tournaments. These are scenario assumptions, not forecasts.

## Purchase-pressure definitions

The model reports three distinct concepts:
- **Ever needed core purchase:** the player could not fund a chosen Ranked ticket from current liquid currency before the modeled purchase decision.
- **Ever needed optional purchase:** the player could not fund a chosen optional tournament/direct/Premium stake from current liquid currency.
- **Simulated purchaser share:** the player actually passed the modeled purchase-propensity roll and received a Crown pack.

The broad “ever needed any purchase” figure is intentionally not a paywall KPI because it includes aspirational optional stakes. Core access pressure is the relevant guardrail.

## Interpretation

The corrected V3.4 economy remains intentionally tight without creating a modeled core paywall:
- only **5.46%** of modeled players ever fall below 25 Coin-equivalent of total liquid wealth;
- **89%** are never blocked from a chosen Ranked ticket over the entire 90-day window;
- **88.7%** never reach a core Ranked purchase decision;
- the median player still finishes with about **1,296 Coin-equivalent**;
- the higher optional purchase-need and tournament-skip figures are expected because Medium/High tournaments, direct stakes and Premium tournaments are discretionary aspirational sinks;
- the simulated purchaser share remains below the 16% balancing ceiling;
- purchased currency still has no input to Elo or rank calculation.

The corrected model therefore does **not** justify increasing recurring rewards again. V3.4 retains the current policy values.

## Guardrails

The simulation fails CI if:
- recurring mint reduction versus the legacy model is less than 25%;
- median wallet exceeds 2,500 Coin-equivalent;
- more than 18% of players ever require a core Ranked purchase decision;
- simulated purchaser share exceeds 16%;
- more than 10% ever fall below 25 Coin-equivalent of liquid wealth;
- more than 15% are ever unable to fund a chosen Ranked ticket;
- more than 50% ever skip a chosen tournament for insufficient liquid currency;
- burned Coin-equivalent is less than 45% of newly minted currency.

Coin-only low-balance pressure is still reported, but it is not used as the starvation guardrail because Crowns are fully liquid at 10:1 and can be converted back to Coins.

These are balancing guardrails, not permanent business KPIs. Real beta telemetry should replace modeled assumptions once there is a meaningful player population.
