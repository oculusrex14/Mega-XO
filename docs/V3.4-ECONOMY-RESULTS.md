# V3.4 Economy Simulation Results

This is a deterministic pressure model for balancing. It is **not** a forecast of retention, conversion rate, ARPU, or revenue.

## Why the V3.3 economy was changed

The previous reward schedule minted currency much faster than the core sinks removed it. In the 90-day model, the old policy produced a median wallet above 10,000 Coin-equivalent and more than 136 newly generated Coins per player per day. That would make currency progressively less meaningful and make the Coin -> Crown exchange too easy to farm at scale.

V3.4 therefore reduced recurring minting before raising any entry fee:
- Starting wallet: **150 Coins**
- Daily quest maximum: **30 Coins**
- Bot daily reward cap: **20 Coins**
- Bot rewarded wins: **3 per difficulty**
- Ranked win bonus: **60% of the league ticket**, rounded down, with a **50 Coin daily cap**
- Weekly league rewards: 20 / 25 / 30 / 40 / 50 / 65 / 85 / 110 / 150 / 220 / 300
- Low tournament: **100 Coins**
- Medium tournament: **400 Coins**
- High tournament: **1,200 Coins**
- Premium tournament: **200 Crowns**
- Coin/Crown exchange remains **10:1 both ways**
- Ranked queue ticket prices are unchanged
- Direct ranked challenge 50/50 payout/burn is unchanged

## Population model

5,000 deterministic modeled players are simulated for 90 days across four behavioral archetypes: light, core, competitive and high-stakes. The model includes Ranked tickets/results, quests, bot rewards, weekly rewards, public tournaments, direct challenges, Premium tournaments and Coin/Crown exchange.

“Modeled purchase pressure” means the model reached a premium/direct-stake decision where a high-intent player could not fund the chosen Crown activity from earned currency. It is deliberately **not called a purchase conversion forecast**.

## Results

| Metric | V3.3-style policy | V3.4 policy |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Coins generated / player / day | 136.39 | **39.23** |
| Coin-equivalent burned / player / day | 26.50 | **25.01** |
| Mint reduction | — | **71.24%** |
| Burn / new-mint ratio | 19.43% | **63.76%** |
| Tournament entry spend / player / 90d | 2,992.86 | **1,196.98** |
| Direct challenge Crowns spent / player / 90d | 36.78 | **37.67** |
| Coins converted to Crowns / player / 90d | 434.51 | **203.01** |
| Wallet p10 after 90d | 5,297 | **822** |
| Wallet median after 90d | 10,208 | **1,302** |
| Wallet p90 after 90d | 15,653 | **2,792** |
| Ever below 25 Coins | 1.44% | **7.32%** |
| Ever unable to fund a chosen Ranked ticket | 3.20% | **12.40%** |
| Ever skip a chosen tournament for insufficient Coins | 21.08% | **42.60%** |
| Modeled purchase pressure | 2.20% | **9.34%** |

## Interpretation

The V3.4 economy is intentionally tighter, but the model does not show a general paywall:
- more than 92% of modeled players never fall below 25 Coins;
- 87.6% are never blocked from a Ranked ticket over the entire 90-day window;
- the median player still finishes with about 1,300 Coin-equivalent;
- the higher tournament skip figure is acceptable because Medium/High tables are optional aspirational stakes, while Low remains the accessible paid tournament;
- modeled purchase pressure rises into a useful single-digit/high-single-digit range without allowing purchased currency to influence Elo.

The current numbers are therefore retained rather than increasing rewards again.

## Guardrails

The simulation fails CI if:
- recurring mint reduction versus the legacy model is less than 25%;
- median wallet exceeds 2,500 Coin-equivalent;
- modeled purchase pressure exceeds 16%;
- more than 10% of players ever fall below 25 Coins;
- more than 15% are ever unable to fund a chosen Ranked ticket;
- more than 50% ever skip a chosen tournament for insufficient Coins;
- burned Coin-equivalent is less than 45% of newly minted currency.

These are balancing guardrails, not permanent business KPIs. Real beta telemetry should replace modeled assumptions once there is a meaningful player population.
