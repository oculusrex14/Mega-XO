# V3.4 Game Design Closure

V3.4 is a game-system hardening release. It does not add match history/replays or puzzles; those are explicitly deferred future features.

## Public tournament fairness

Public Low, Medium, High and Premium tournaments keep the V3.3.2 matchmaking rule: the final ten-player cohort has a hard maximum **200 Elo spread**. Older references to 400 Elo are stale.

## Ranked seasons

Mega XO uses **quarterly ranked seasons**: Jan-Mar, Apr-Jun, Jul-Sep and Oct-Dec, rolling at 00:00 UTC on the first day of each quarter.

Why quarterly:
- monthly resets are appropriate for high-volume progression games but too frequent for a slower strategy title;
- annual resets let inactive elite players occupy prestige too long;
- quarterly is long enough to build a meaningful record while still creating four competitive refreshes per year.

Research inputs:
- Clash Royale resets Ranked every month and gives returning high finishers a win multiplier: https://support.supercell.com/clash-royale/en/articles/ranked-3.html
- VALORANT has used abridged placement matches while referencing prior skill, rather than throwing away all prior skill information: https://playvalorant.com/en-us/news/dev/act-ii-ranks-and-competitive-changes/
- Clash of Clans' current Ranked/Legend systems explicitly demote for prolonged ranked inactivity: https://supercell.com/en/games/clashofclans/blog/release-notes/the-sound-of-clash-update/ and https://support.supercell.com/clash-of-clans/en/articles/legend-league-4.html

### Season reset model

Mega XO keeps underlying Elo continuity. A season reset clears **season qualification, seasonal record, seasonal peak and leaderboard participation**, not learned skill.

At a new season:
1. archive the prior season summary;
2. keep the player's Elo as the skill prior;
3. require **5 rated requalification games** before the player is published on the skill leaderboard or allocated an elite seat;
4. at least **3 of the 5** must be matchmade Ranked queue games;
5. at least **3 unique rated opponents** are required.

This combines the useful part of VALORANT-style requalification with the "return quickly to your real level" idea behind Clash Royale's rank boost, without creating a fake grind or resetting a Grandmaster to beginner skill.

### Elite-seat activity

Champion, Master and Grandmaster are scarce seats, so they cannot be parked indefinitely.

To hold an elite seat at each weekly publication:
- season must be qualified;
- at least **5 rated games in the last 14 days**;
- at least **3 matchmade Ranked games** in the last 14 days;
- at least **3 unique rated opponents** in the last 14 days;
- at least one rated game in the last **7 days**.

Direct ranked challenges count toward total rated activity, but **direct challenges are never mandatory**. Requiring a paid direct challenge to retain rank would couple prestige to spending and create collusion/harassment incentives.

If an elite player becomes inactive, the scarce seat is released at the next weekly publication. Their Elo is not destroyed. They display at the highest open league their Elo supports until they requalify for an elite seat.

Non-elite accounts keep their Elo but disappear from public skill leaderboards after 28 days without a rated match. Returning players resume from the same skill prior.

## Economy targets

The V3.4 economy should satisfy these product goals:
- Casual, friend-free and offline local play remain free.
- Ranked should be affordable from normal play, but not a net Coin printer.
- Daily rewards should support activity without making Coin -> Crown conversion effectively unlimited.
- Weekly rank rewards should be meaningful but not dominate all other earning.
- Public tournaments remain strong sinks because 10% of each tournament pool is retired.
- Direct ranked challenges remain 50% burn / 50% winner payout, challenger-funded.
- Buying Crowns changes wealth and access to high-stake play, never Elo.

V3.4 adds an executable population simulation and tunes rewards against:
- Coins generated/player/day
- Coins burned
- tournament entry spending
- direct challenge spending
- Coin/Crown conversion
- wallet distribution
- percent of players hitting low-wallet states
- percent of modeled players who would need a purchase to sustain their chosen premium/direct-stake behavior

Purchase rate in the simulation is a **modeled pressure metric**, not a forecast of real conversion.

## Tournament records

Profiles/statistics gain aggregate tournament performance only:
- tournaments entered
- tournament wins
- runner-up finishes
- top 3
- top 5
- best finish
- average finish
- premium tournament wins

Tournament results continue to leave normal Ranked Elo unchanged.

## Explicitly deferred

### Match history and replay
Deferred to a future monetized update. V3.4 must not accidentally expose a paid replay product or alter existing short recent-game lists.

### Puzzles
Deferred to a future update. Candidate feature: daily "find the winning route" tactical positions, potentially generated from real matches.

## Invariants

V3.4 adds executable game-design invariants covering:
- Mega Board send rule and free-route rule
- Mini/Mega win resolution
- Elo never depends on money or stake size
- Ranked queue has equal Coin contributions
- direct ranked challenges are challenger-funded
- 50/50 direct-challenge retirement
- tournament prize conservation and fifth-place break-even
- public tournament 200-Elo hard cohort cap
- seasonal requalification / elite inactivity rules
- currency conversion conservation
- server authority over balances, results and opponent selection
