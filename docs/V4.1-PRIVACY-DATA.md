# V4.1 privacy and personal-data plumbing

V4.1 adds a player-facing **Download my data** export while keeping destructive account deletion disabled until Antimatter Innovations approves the privacy/retention policy.

## Export security

The export endpoint is POST-only, same-origin/CSRF protected, requires a linked account and recent reauthentication within 15 minutes, is rate-limited per account, and is additionally production-IP-throttled.

The browser turns the returned JSON into a local file named like `mega-xo-data-YYYY-MM-DD.json`.

## Included data

The export contains the player's own:

- stable player ID/tag, username/display name/avatar and visibility settings;
- verified email address and timestamps;
- linked identity provider subjects and link timestamps;
- wallet balances, owned items and monetisation state;
- rating/rank/casual/season/tournament records;
- match history and daily progression records;
- friends, incoming/outgoing requests and blocked-player references as player tag/username references;
- economy journal entries belonging to that player;
- verified store receipt records belonging to that player;
- cloud practice save;
- reports submitted by that player, including review outcome when available.

## Explicitly excluded

The export never includes:

- password hashes or salts;
- OTP codes/hashes/challenges;
- CSRF values or session cookies/token hashes;
- IP pseudonym/rate-limit buckets;
- private operator audit records or operator reasons;
- reports filed by other players against the account;
- other players' email addresses or provider identifiers;
- server secrets or provider credentials.

## Moderation/report access

Reports **submitted by** the player are included. Reports **about** the player are not exposed because they may contain reporter or moderation information. Final access/retention treatment is part of the approved moderation/privacy policy.

## Deletion state machine scaffold

Migration `v41-privacy-requests` reserves a durable deletion workflow with states:

```text
requested -> verified -> processing -> completed
                         \-> failed
requested/verified -> cancelled
```

No public deletion endpoint is exposed in V4.1 and production configuration rejects `MEGA_ACCOUNT_DELETION_ENABLED=true` with `ACCOUNT_DELETION_POLICY_NOT_APPROVED`.

This prevents accidental partial deletion before the company has decided what must be deleted, anonymised, or legally retained.

## Policy decisions required before deletion can ship

Antimatter Innovations must approve:

- Privacy Policy and Terms of Service;
- complete personal-data inventory;
- legitimate retention requirements and durations for security/fraud/purchase records;
- treatment of matches, ratings, leaderboards, social relationships, receipts and moderation records;
- provider-token/account revocation behavior;
- external deletion-request page required for Google Play distribution;
- support/escalation process for deletion disputes or failed requests.

Those decisions remain tracked under `EXT-21` in `docs/V4-OPEN-BLOCKERS.md`.

## Acceptance

Before release, staging should prove:

1. a recently authenticated player can download the export;
2. an old session gets `REAUTH_REQUIRED`;
3. exported JSON contains expected account/social/economy/save data;
4. password/session/OTP/operator secrets are absent;
5. rate limiting works;
6. deletion remains unavailable until the approved policy is implemented.
