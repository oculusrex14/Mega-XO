# V4.1 Tasks 21-24 repository checkpoint

This checkpoint records repository-side completion. It does not claim that Antimatter Innovations legal/policy approval, Play Console/App Store Connect setup, native SDK integration, or physical-device acceptance has been completed.

## Task 21 - privacy and account deletion

- policy-version-gated destructive deletion;
- recent reauthentication and exact immutable MEGA-tag confirmation;
- deletion blocked during active match, queue or room work;
- immediate session/identity/profile/email/cloud/social cleanup;
- reports submitted by the player removed and reports about the player stripped of free text/pseudonymized;
- historical match/economy/purchase integrity references pseudonymized;
- minimal deletion receipt retained without email/tag/raw actor ID;
- stable public `/delete-account` resource;
- deleted identities may create a new profile but cannot restore the former tag;
- store account bindings are removed during deletion.

External policy/publication/live acceptance remains EXT-21.

## Task 22 - Google Play Billing

- stable server-generated Play account binding;
- Google service-account OAuth boundary;
- ProductPurchaseV2 backend verification;
- exact package/product/quantity/purchase-state/account checks;
- immutable mapping from store SKUs to the four server catalogue products;
- idempotent grant/replay protection;
- post-commit consume/acknowledge finalization with retry;
- authenticated Pub/Sub push verification;
- RTDN and voided-purchase refund/revocation processing;
- production secret/config/deployment plumbing.

Real Play Console products, Android Billing client and device sandbox proof remain EXT-22.

## Task 23 - Apple StoreKit

- stable server-generated StoreKit app-account token;
- signed transaction JWS verification;
- ES256 signature and x5c chain validation against configured Apple roots;
- exact bundle/environment/product/quantity/transaction/account-token checks;
- idempotent grant/replay/revocation protection;
- App Store Server Notifications V2 verification, dedupe and refund/revocation handling;
- trusted-root production secret/config/deployment plumbing.

Real App Store Connect products, StoreKit 2 client and Sandbox/TestFlight proof remain EXT-23.

## Task 24 - ads and consent

- production AdMob SSV cryptographic verifier wired into runtime;
- rewarded tickets are bound to exact platform and rewarded ad unit;
- SSV must match ticket, actor, unit, reward and provider transaction before grant;
- provider transaction/ticket replay protection;
- automatic interstitial permits are bound to exact platform/interstitial unit;
- production ads require enabled approved privacy/deletion policy, explicit consent-release version and complete native unit configuration;
- `off` remains the default;
- browser UI requires native consent readiness and cannot grant from a local watched callback;
- Android/iOS billing, ads, UMP and SSV native bridge contract is documented in `native/COMMERCE-AND-ADS.md`.

Real AdMob apps/units, Google Mobile Ads + UMP integration and physical-device/region acceptance remain EXT-24.

## Release posture

Purchases and ads are independent feature gates. Repository support does not authorize enabling either capability. `MEGA_PURCHASES_ENABLED=false` and `MEGA_AD_MODE=off` remain appropriate until their external blockers are complete. Paid entry remains separately hard-disabled.
