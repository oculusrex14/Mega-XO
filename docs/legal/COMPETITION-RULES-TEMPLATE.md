## 1. Eligibility

State all of the following explicitly:

- minimum verified age;
- permitted countries/states/territories;
- residency/location requirements;
- account-good-standing requirements;
- employee/contractor/household exclusions if applicable;
- sanctions/KYC or identity requirements where applicable;
- one-person/one-account requirements;
- any jurisdiction-specific exclusions.

Eligibility is determined by server-side controls. Profile country, locale, store country or a self-attested checkbox is not sufficient where trusted location/age verification is required.

## 2. Entry

For each competition class disclose:

- fixed entry/registration fee;
- currency/payment rail;
- maximum entries per person;
- entry window;
- cancellation/refund deadline;
- whether the fee covers administration/participation only;
- statement that entrant fees do **not** form the prize pool;
- statement that purchased Mega XO Crowns/derived Coins are not accepted for competition entry.

No side bets, variable stakes, challenger-created pots, auto-re-entry or auto-top-up are permitted.

## 3. Prize schedule

Publish the complete prize schedule before entry:

- placement;
- prize;
- prize type;
- sponsor/organizer funding source;
- tie treatment;
- tax withholding/reporting treatment where applicable;
- claim/verification requirements;
- unclaimed-prize treatment.

For the current P1-7 technical baseline, prizes must not be cash, cash-equivalent or otherwise redeemable for real-world value unless a later legal/platform review and reviewed code change expressly approves that model.

## 4. Competition format

Define:

- Mega XO rules version;
- bracket/group format;
- number of participants;
- seeding method;
- matchmaking/rating window;
- clocks/time controls;
- tie-break method;
- server-authoritative result source;
- event schedule;
- technical pause/resume policy.

No outcome may depend on amount paid or purchased currency.

## 5. Fair play

Prohibit:

- collusion or intentional result manipulation;
- multiple accounts used to gain additional entries or coordinate outcomes;
- bots, solvers, scripts or prohibited assistance;
- account sharing;
- exploiting bugs or desynchronization;
- network manipulation;
- harassment or coercion;
- falsifying age/location/identity;
- payment fraud or chargeback abuse.

State the evidence standard and escalation path used before disqualification or suspension.

## 6. Technical failures, cancellation and refunds

Define exactly what happens for:

- server outage before start;
- server outage during play;
- client disconnect;
- no-show;
- event cancellation;
- corrupted/void result;
- duplicate payment/entry;
- participant disqualification;
- refund/revocation/chargeback.

Refunds and settlements must be exactly-once and server-authoritative.

## 7. Spending and player protections

Disclose applicable:

- maximum entry fee;
- daily entry-spend cap;
- daily/weekly loss cap where applicable;
- cooling-off controls;
- self-exclusion;
- account holds;
- support/escalation route.

Do not use loss-chasing, urgency or misleading "win back" language.

## 8. Location and age verification

Explain that eligibility may require:

- trusted age verification;
- trusted country/state determination;
- re-checks at entry or other legally required times;
- denial when location is unavailable, conflicting or high-risk;
- denial when age verification is absent/stale.

Do not disclose details that would help users defeat anti-fraud/geolocation controls.

## 9. Disputes and appeals

State:

- how to open a dispute;
- deadline;
- information the participant should provide;
- evidence considered;
- review authority;
- appeal route;
- expected decision/communication process.

Never ask users to send passwords, OTPs, cookies or full payment credentials.

## 10. Privacy

Link the effective Mega XO Privacy Policy and explain any competition-specific processing, including age/location/KYC or prize/tax data.

The effective policy and retention schedule must cover the exact competition data before launch.

## 11. Platform notices

### Apple

If distributed through Apple's App Store, include the approved wording making clear that Apple is not a sponsor of, or involved in, the competition. Ensure the actual model complies with current App Review Guideline 5.3 and any product-specific App Review decision.

### Google

If distributed through Google Play, ensure the actual model is permitted under the current Real-Money Gambling, Games and Contests policy and any product-specific Google approval. Do not imply Google sponsors or endorses the competition.

## 12. Governing law and mandatory rights

Insert only counsel-approved language for:

- governing law;
- venue/dispute forum;
- mandatory consumer rights;
- regulator complaint rights;
- tax reporting.

Do not copy a generic jurisdiction clause across countries.

## 13. Change control

After entry opens, material competition terms must not be changed to a participant's detriment except where the approved rules/law expressly allow it.

Every active competition must store or reference the immutable rules version accepted at entry.
