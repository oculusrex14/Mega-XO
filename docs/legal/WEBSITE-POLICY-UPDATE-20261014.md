## Official research checked 14 October 2026

- [Information Technology (Reasonable security practices and procedures and sensitive personal data or information) Rules, 2011, official Gazette text hosted by WIPO](https://www.wipo.int/wipolex/en/text/494931): Rules 4–8 cover clear practices, purpose limitation, retention, correction/withdrawal, grievances, disclosure, transfers and security. Rule 5(3) requires the collector/retainer's name/address disclosure, and Rule 5(9) requires a named Grievance Officer/contact and grievance resolution within one month.
- [Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, India Code amended compilation](https://www.indiacode.nic.in/bitstream/123456789/22037/1/a2023-22.pdf): the official indexed commencement note distinguishes immediate provisions from substantive provisions scheduled eighteen months after 13 November 2025. Do not describe every DPDP obligation as already effective on 14 October 2026. The Act defines a child as under 18 and contains purpose/notice/consent, safeguards and individual-rights provisions. PDF full fetch timed out; indexed official text and MeitY publication were used without claiming a full PDF review.
- [MeitY's DPDP Rules, 2025 publication](https://www.meity.gov.in/documents/act-and-policies/digital-personal-data-protection-rules-2025-gDOxUjMtQWa?pageTitle=Digital-Personal-Data-Protection-Rules-2025): current official publication, distinct from the January 2025 consultation draft.
- [Indian Contract Act, 1872, India Code](https://www.indiacode.nic.in/handle/123456789/2187?col=123456789%2F1362): contract capacity and mandatory rights inform account eligibility and the absence of an invented exclusive venue.
- [MeitY's Promotion and Regulation of Online Gaming Act/rules publication](https://www.meity.gov.in/documents/act-and-policies/promotion-andregulation-of-online-gaming-act-2025-and-its-corrigenda-kTMxQjMtQWa): distinguishes social games from online money games.
