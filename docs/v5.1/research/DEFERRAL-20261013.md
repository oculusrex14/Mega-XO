until real user gameplay data has been collected.

## Why deferring is the right call

The research showed the achievable strength is bounded by **label quality**, and the strongest labels
came from an external MCTS corpus — not from our own games. Once **real human gameplay data** exists,
it becomes the highest-value supervision source: real players explore positions, tactics and mistakes
that no self-play corpus covers, and it is exactly the data the deferred programme lacks. Deferring
until then avoids burning compute on synthetic self-play that the measurements already show plateaus.

## When training resumes

Starting points are all committed and reproducible: the corrected pipeline, the converter + multi-source
corpus builder, the arena, the anchored RL loop, the head-to-head gate, and the evidence set. The first
step becomes: ingest real gameplay records through the same canonical-rules validation, convert them as
a new `valueTargetSource`, and re-run the anchored multi-generation loop with that anchor.
