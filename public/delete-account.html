Delete your Mega XOXO account

You can permanently delete your Mega XOXO account from Mega XOXO on the web or in a supported app. You must sign in and verify your identity before deletion.

Delete from Mega XOXO

  1. Open Mega XOXO and sign in to the profile you want to delete.
  2. Open Your profile, then Linked accounts & recovery.
  3. Under Privacy, choose Delete account.
  4. Type your exact player tag and confirm permanent deletion.
Open Mega XOXO

What deletion does

Deletion removes the account's sign-in methods, profile, cloud practice save, social connections and active account data. Historical records that must be retained for purchase integrity, fraud/security, moderation or legal obligations may be retained only in pseudonymized or minimum-record form according to the applicable Privacy Policy and approved retention schedule.

Deletion is permanent. A later sign-in with the same email, Google account or Apple account creates or links a new Mega XOXO profile; it does not restore the deleted profile.
