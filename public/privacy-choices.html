Privacy choices

Draft release-support page · 2026-10-07

Mega XO provides account controls directly in the product. Some controls require recent reauthentication to protect your account.

Download your data

Open your signed-in profile/privacy controls and choose Download my data. The export is allowlisted and excludes password hashes, OTPs, session secrets, private operator records and reports submitted by other players about you.

Delete your account

Open your signed-in profile and choose Delete account, or review the instructions at /delete-account. Deletion requires recent identity verification and exact confirmation of your player tag.

Profile and social controls

You can update profile visibility, manage friends and blocks, and control supported account/profile preferences from Mega XO.

Sessions and sign-in methods

You can review active sessions, revoke other sessions and link or unlink supported sign-in providers when account-security rules allow it.

Advertising privacy

The current production baseline keeps advertising disabled until the native advertising/consent release gate is complete. If ads are enabled in a later build, the app must expose the applicable consent/privacy-options surface before ad requests are allowed where required.

Other privacy requests

If a privacy request cannot be completed through the available controls, contact contact@antimatterinnovations.com. Do not include your password, OTP, session cookie or full store receipt.

This page summarizes product controls. The final effective Privacy Policy and jurisdiction-specific rights still require formal approval before launch.
