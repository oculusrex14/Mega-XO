Privacy Policy

Draft version: 2026-10-07 · Not yet effective

Draft for legal review. This document describes the current V4.1 implementation and planned launch configuration. It must not be treated as the final production privacy policy until Antimatter Innovations approves the policy version and removes this notice.

This Privacy Policy explains how Antimatter Innovations handles information when you use Mega XOXO, including its web experience and supported mobile apps.

1. Information we handle

Account and profile information

If you create or link an account, we may process your email address, sign-in provider identifier, Mega XOXO player ID/tag, username/display name, avatar and privacy/profile preferences. Passwords are stored only as password hashes and salts; one-time verification codes are stored only in protected verification form and expire quickly.

Gameplay and social information

We process game saves, match records, ratings, rank/season records, tournament records, wallet/virtual-item state, friends, requests, blocks, matchmaking state and similar gameplay data needed to provide the game. If you submit a player report, we process the selected category and any report detail you choose to provide.

Purchases and advertising

The current production baseline keeps store purchases and advertising disabled until their separate release gates are complete. If enabled in a supported release, we may process store product identifiers, transaction/receipt identifiers, entitlement and refund/revocation state, advertising consent state, ad-unit identifiers and reward-verification records. Apple, Google and advertising providers may separately process information under their own policies. We do not receive full payment-card or bank-account details from the app stores.

Technical, security and support information

We process operational information needed to secure and run Mega XOXO, such as short-lived session and CSRF values, rate-limit/security buckets derived from network information, normalized request route/status information, opaque MX-… support codes, service health information and security/operator audit records. Raw passwords, OTPs, cookies, email contents and purchase receipts are not written to the support-correlation index.

2. Why we use information

  • Provide accounts, authentication, cloud saves, multiplayer, matchmaking, social features and support.
  • Maintain ratings, virtual balances, purchase entitlements and fraud/replay protection.
  • Secure the service, rate-limit abuse, investigate incidents and enforce game/community rules.
  • Send requested verification, password-reset and security emails.
  • Comply with lawful obligations and enforce our Terms.
  • If advertising is enabled, request consent where required and deliver/verify allowed ads and rewards.

3. Service providers

Depending on the enabled release features, Antimatter Innovations may use service providers for hosting, encrypted backups, transactional email, sign-in, app-store billing, advertising/consent and availability monitoring. Planned/current providers include Oracle Cloud infrastructure, Cloudflare R2 for encrypted off-site backup storage, Resend for transactional email, Google services for Google sign-in/Google Play/AdMob where enabled, and Apple services for Sign in with Apple/App Store purchases where enabled. The final production provider list must be reviewed before this policy becomes effective.

4. Browser storage and cookies

Mega XOXO uses a first-party session cookie for account sessions. In production it is configured as Secure, HttpOnly and SameSite=Lax. The web client also uses local browser storage for functional settings and offline/practice state. The current web baseline does not intentionally deploy third-party marketing cookies or web advertising trackers. Any future non-essential tracking or advertising technology must pass a separate consent and disclosure review before enablement.

5. Retention

We keep information only for the period needed for the purposes above, subject to the approved retention schedule. Current implementation automatically expires or bounds short-lived authentication, session, presence, email-delivery and support-correlation records. Core profile/game/social/cloud-save data is generally held while the account exists. When an account is deleted, active identity/profile/social/cloud data is removed and historical records are deleted or pseudonymized where they are not required for purchase integrity, fraud/security, moderation or legal obligations.

Final durations for purchase-integrity records, operator/security audit records, moderation outcomes and deletion receipts require formal policy/legal approval before this draft becomes effective.

6. Your choices and controls

  • Update profile and visibility settings in Mega XOXO.
  • Review and revoke other active sessions.
  • Link or unlink supported sign-in providers, subject to account-security requirements.
  • Download an allowlisted copy of your account data after recent reauthentication.
  • Delete your account from the authenticated in-app privacy controls.
  • Use the public account deletion page for deletion instructions.
  • Use Privacy choices for an overview of available controls.

7. Account deletion

Account deletion is permanent. It removes sign-in methods, profile, cloud practice save, social relationships and other active account data. Records that must be retained for legitimate security, fraud, purchase-integrity, moderation or legal reasons are reduced to the minimum needed and are pseudonymized where the implementation supports it. A later sign-in with the same external identity creates or links a new profile; it does not restore the deleted profile.

8. Security

We use technical and organizational safeguards designed to protect data, including encrypted HTTPS transport, secure session cookies, password hashing, bounded authentication attempts, encrypted pending email payloads, encrypted off-site backups, least-exposure operator interfaces and audited incident-response controls. No system can guarantee absolute security.

9. International processing

Infrastructure and service providers may process information in countries other than your own. The final production policy must document the applicable transfer safeguards and provider terms for the launch jurisdictions.

10. Children and age eligibility

The final minimum age, parental-consent approach and child-directed distribution position have not yet been approved. Mega XOXO must not be marketed or configured as a child-directed service until that review is complete.

11. Changes

We will update the effective policy when our data practices materially change. Store privacy disclosures must be kept consistent with the effective policy and the actual released app.

12. Contact

Privacy questions may be sent to contact@antimatterinnovations.com. The final policy will identify any additional legally required controller/entity address or privacy contact before launch.
