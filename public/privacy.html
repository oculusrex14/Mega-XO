Privacy Policy

Mega XOXO is a hobby and experimental strategy-game project operated by Antimatter Innovations, an India-based sole proprietorship. This policy explains what we handle, why we need it and how you can make a privacy request. It covers the website at megaxo.online, its development previews and supported Mega XOXO apps.

Your privacy at a glance

  • Local practice does not require an account. Online play uses an account to keep your games, profile and progression together.
  • We do not sell your personal information. This website currently runs no advertising network or marketing trackers.
  • Profile visibility is your choice. Your email, credentials and session secrets are not public leaderboard information.
  • You can request access, correction or deletion through the available account controls or our contact email.

1. Information we handle

Accounts and authentication

When you create or link an account, we handle your email, account and player identifiers, sign-in-provider identifiers where supported, verification and recovery records, and session information. We store password hashes and salts rather than readable passwords. Verification codes expire; they are used to establish account access, not for marketing.

Profiles, games and social features

We handle your username, display name, avatar and visibility preferences; saved practice progress; match moves and results; ratings, leagues, seasons and tournament records; Coins, Crowns and virtual-item ownership; and friends, requests, blocks and online presence. These records let the game show the correct board, enforce fair play, settle matches and keep your account consistent. If you report a player or contact support, we also handle the information you choose to provide.

Technical and security information

Our infrastructure receives network information such as your IP address and browser request details when you connect. We use session records, request status and timing, abuse-prevention counters, service logs and security audit records to operate and protect the service. Infrastructure providers may maintain their own operational logs. Support codes beginning with MX- help us investigate a problem without asking for your password or session cookie.

Purchases and advertising

Purchases and advertising are currently inactive. If a supported release later offers them, we will explain the relevant provider and choices before use. Store fulfilment may require product and transaction identifiers, entitlement status, and refund or revocation records. Stores handle payment details; we do not receive your full payment-card or bank-account details from them. Optional advertising will require the appropriate disclosures and consent or opt-out controls.

2. How we use information

  • Authenticate you, recover your account and send requested verification or security messages.
  • Provide saves, multiplayer, matchmaking, friends, challenges and tournaments.
  • Maintain authoritative ratings, virtual balances and ownership, and prevent duplicate or fraudulent actions.
  • Investigate reports, answer support requests, secure the service and meet applicable legal obligations.

We use information for the purposes described here. Where consent is required, we request it separately; agreeing to the Terms is not consent to unrelated tracking. You can withdraw an optional choice without losing local practice. Withdrawing information needed for authentication or online play may prevent us from providing those features.

3. Who receives information

Other players see the information needed for a match or social interaction, such as your display name, player tag and game actions. Public profiles and leaderboards follow the applicable visibility and publication settings; wealth publication has a separate opt-in. We do not publish account email addresses or authentication information.

We use infrastructure providers including Vercel for website/API hosting, Oracle Cloud for game services, Neon for account and game databases, and Upstash for short-lived service coordination. Only the information needed for their service is provided. Backup storage, email delivery, identity and app-store providers are used where those features are configured. A provider you choose for sign-in or purchases also handles information under its own policy.

Access within Antimatter Innovations is limited to operating, securing and supporting the project. We may disclose the minimum necessary information when legally required, to protect users or investigate abuse. We do not provide player contact information to sponsors for their marketing.

4. Cookies, preferences and offline practice

We use first-party session cookies to keep you signed in and protect account requests. Secure deployments use HTTPS and HttpOnly session cookies. Browser storage holds functional preferences and local practice state. An offline copy is saved only when you choose that option in Settings; you can remove it there or through your browser.

Sponsor-interest preferences are optional and stay in your browser after you agree to save them. Saving those interests does not enable third-party tracking. Clear them on the Sponsors page. This website currently uses no third-party marketing cookies. External links open services with their own privacy practices.

5. How long information is kept

We keep account, profile, game and social information while it is needed to provide your account and the features you use. Sessions expire; linked sessions normally last up to 14 days and anonymous sessions up to 24 hours. Verification challenges have a short expiry and cannot be reused after completion. Expiry prevents further use; cleanup of stored records may occur later.

When you delete an account, sign-in access is revoked and active identity, profile, save and social information is removed through the deletion process. Minimum historical match, integrity, security, moderation or legally required records may remain for the purpose that requires them, with personal references removed or replaced where possible. Pseudonymous records can still be personal data if they can be linked back to someone.

Older encrypted recovery backups can contain information deleted from the active service until those backups are retired. They are restricted to recovery and must not be used to restore a deleted account to active use. If we retain information after a deletion request, you can ask us which categories remain and why. Retention for a legal dispute or investigation is limited to that need.

6. Your controls and privacy requests

Use your Profile to edit supported details and visibility, your Account for sign-in and recovery, and Privacy choices for data access and deletion instructions. Sensitive actions may require recent identity verification. If a control is unavailable in your client, email us instead.

You can ask to access or correct your information, request an export or deletion, withdraw optional consent, or raise a privacy complaint. We verify that a request concerns your account without asking you to disclose a password or verification code. We aim to respond within 30 days, or sooner where law requires, and will explain a delay or any lawful reason we cannot fulfil a request. You retain any additional rights provided by the law that applies to you, including the right to complain to the competent authority.

Account deletion is permanent and does not restore the old profile if you later sign up again. See Delete account before proceeding. Removing this website's browser storage alone does not delete a server account.

7. Children

Mega XOXO does not offer child accounts or a parental-verification service. Account holders must be adults legally able to agree to the Terms. Do not submit a child's personal information or create an account on a child's behalf. If you believe we have received such information, contact us so we can investigate and arrange its removal. We do not target advertising to children.

8. Security and international processing

We use safeguards such as HTTPS, password hashing, access restrictions, bounded authentication attempts and encrypted recovery material. These measures reduce risk but cannot guarantee that every incident will be prevented. If a personal-data incident occurs, we will investigate, take corrective action and provide notices required by applicable law.

Our providers may process information outside India or your country. We limit transfers to what the service needs and apply the protections required by applicable law. Contact us for information about providers and processing locations relevant to your account.

9. Gameplay research and future changes

The project researches game-playing AI using controlled research data. The proposed system for collecting player gameplay for training is not enabled by this policy. If we introduce that use, we will first provide a specific notice explaining the data, purpose, retention and available choices, and obtain consent where required. We do not send account credentials or contact information into training datasets.

We will update this policy when our practices change and give notice of material changes through the website or the relevant account flow. New optional processing will not be enabled merely by changing this text.

10. Contact

For privacy questions, requests or grievances, email Antimatter Innovations at contact@antimatterinnovations.com. Include the nature of your request and, where useful, your player tag or support code. Do not send passwords, verification codes, session cookies or full payment details.
