Support

Support and privacy contact

Contact

Email contact@antimatterinnovations.com.

Please do not send passwords, OTPs, session cookies, full payment receipts, private keys or other secrets.

Include a support code when available

If Mega XO shows an error code beginning with MX-, include that code in your message. It lets support find a sanitized request record without needing your password, OTP, cookie, email contents or raw receipt.

Account access

For sign-in or recovery problems, use the built-in forgot-password or provider sign-in flow first. Support will not ask you to reveal a password or OTP.

Privacy and account data

Signed-in players can download their data from Mega XO after recent reauthentication. For deletion, use the in-app Delete account control or see Delete your Mega XO account. Other privacy choices are listed at Privacy choices.

Purchases

If store purchases are enabled in your build, include the store platform, product name and approximate purchase time. Do not email full card details. Refund eligibility may depend on Apple/Google store processes and applicable law.

Safety and abuse reports

Use the in-product report feature for player conduct when possible so the report is associated with the correct player and fixed category. For account-security concerns, contact the address above.

Response expectations

Final public support response-time commitments and escalation/appeal rules will be defined in the approved support and moderation policy before serious public launch.
